## Accounts

- Homie works on one phone with no account at all. An account — an email

- Use an address you can receive mail at, and keep your password to
  yourself. You're responsible for what happens under your account.
- One account is one person. Don't create accounts in bulk or by automated
  means.
- Confirming your email address switches on Homie's smarter reader (see
  below). Until then, Homie reads what you type on the phone itself.

## Households and the people in them

- The person who creates a household looks after it: they can invite
  people, set what each account can see and change, and hand the household
  over to someone else.
- **Children can't sign up on their own.** A child's account can only join a
  household through an invite created by an adult in that household.
  Creating that invite is the parent's or guardian's consent to the child
  using Homie, and the adults of the household are responsible for how a
  child's account is used.
- Only invite people who have agreed to be part of your household, and only
  add information about others that you're entitled to share with them.

## Subscriptions

- **Homie is free on one phone, for as long as you like.** Keeping a
  household in step across more than one account's phones is the part with a
  price.
- The first month of sharing is free. It starts when a second person joins
  your household, and it's yours rather than the household's: you get one
  free month, not one per household.
- After that, the household's creator subscribes through Apple's App Store,
  monthly or for six or twelve months at a time. One subscription covers
  every phone in the household. Nobody helping out in someone else's
  household is ever asked to pay.
- **Payment is charged to your Apple account** when you confirm the
  purchase. The subscription **renews automatically** for the same length
  and price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end
  of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within
  the 24 hours before that period ends.
- You can change or cancel at any time from Homie (Home → Subscription →
  Manage subscription) or in your device's App Store subscription settings.
  Cancelling stops the next renewal; the household keeps what it paid for
  until the end of the current period.
- Prices are shown in your own currency before you buy. We may change
  prices; Apple will tell you before a change applies to an existing
  subscription, and you can cancel before it does.
- **Refunds are handled by Apple** under Apple's policies — we can't issue
  them directly. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
- If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted. Your household's plans, lists
  and places stay on every phone and stay readable; changes and syncing

## The smarter reader

- When you capture a plan in words, Homie can send that text to Anthropic's
  Claude model, through our own server, to turn it into a draft. Our privacy
  policy explains what is sent and what isn't; the text is not used to train
  AI models.
- **Drafts can be wrong.** A date, time, person or place may be misread.
  Nothing is saved to your schedule until you've reviewed it and confirmed
  it, and you're responsible for what you confirm.
- There is a daily limit per account, and the smarter reader may sometimes
  be unavailable. When it is, Homie reads on the phone instead.

and a reminder can fail to arrive. Use your own judgement, especially for
anything time-critical, and never rely on Homie where safety is at stake.
